Privacy Policy

Effective August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Mission Galaxy ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use this website or request game-development updates. Mission Galaxy is an independent game development of Advisors Group International Inc and Town4 Media Group.

Information we collect

  • Information you provide — your email address and any optional preferences you mark on the launch list (for example, a request to be notified when a Discord founders channel opens).
  • Service information — technical details reasonably necessary to operate this website and deliver the updates you request (such as security checks on the form).

Phone numbers and SMS

This website does not collect phone numbers and does not operate a text-messaging program. Mission Galaxy is not part of an A2P 10DLC or similar carrier-registered SMS campaign, and it does not have a phone number assigned for consumer messaging. Launch updates are sent by email only.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • add you to the Mission Galaxy launch list;
  • send email updates about the game, demo, and public window;
  • notify you if you asked to be pinged when a Discord founders channel opens; and
  • operate, secure, and improve this website.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us operate this website and deliver email (for example, hosting and communications-delivery providers), and only as needed for those purposes, or where required by law.

Data retention and security

We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above reflects the latest revision.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Use the launch-list form at https://missiongalaxy.com/.
